Welcome To Wisconsin University

WIUC was founded in 1998 with full accreditation in 2000 by the National Accreditation Board (NAB). It was the first private university to run an MBA programme in Ghana with nine (9) International MBA students under Rev. David Asante Dartey, as the Director, and Togbe Kwao Anipati IV, as Dean of Academic Studies. A suburban top-class university within the educational enclave, with modern infrastructure, serene and conducive environment for living, learning, playing and networking. Wisconsin now offers an educational experience that produces enterprising graduates. A multicultural and diverse environment with students spanning over 40 nationalities.

Core Values

  • Integrity: Our operations shall always be governed by the highest level of integrity, ethical standards and openness.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Clients/customers/students are the basis for the existence of the University. Their ultimate satisfaction is most paramount in whatever we do.
  • Transparency & Accountability: We are accountable and transparent to our students and other stakeholders.
